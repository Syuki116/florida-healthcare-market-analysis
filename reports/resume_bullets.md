# Resume Bullets

- Integrated two annual Florida county-health releases into a 67-county analytical dataset using Python and FIPS-based joins, calculating population growth and standardizing demographic, disease-burden, provider-supply, and income KPIs.
- Built a transparent percentile-based market opportunity model with three strategic scenarios, identifying six counties that remained in the Top 10 across Balanced, Healthcare Need, and Growth weightings.
- Developed a SQLite analysis layer and Streamlit dashboard using CTEs, CASE logic, window-function rankings, interactive comparisons, and executive recommendations for outpatient-clinic market screening.
